About Me

I am Md. Forhad Babu, an undergraduate student of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, with an average CGPA of 3.65 (out of 4.00).
My research interests include Renewable Energy, Battery and Energy Storage, Electrochemistry, Nanotechnology, Computational Chemistry (DFT using Gaussian), and Bioinformatics. I am particularly passionate about exploring sustainable and innovative solutions in the energy sector through advanced chemical and computational approaches.
I have practical experience with Gaussian, GaussView, OriginPro, MS Office along with strong leadership, teamwork, and research enthusiasm. I am always eager to learn, collaborate, and contribute to impactful scientific research.
