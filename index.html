I am Md. Forhad Babu, a final-year undergraduate student of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, with a CGPA of 3.65 out of 4.00.

My research interests lie at the frontier of renewable energy, electrochemistry, nanotechnology, and computational chemistry. I am passionate about exploring sustainable solutions in the energy sector through advanced chemical and computational approaches including DFT simulations.
