About Me

Passionate about

I am Md. Forhad Babu, a final-year undergraduate student of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, maintaining a CGPA of 3.65 out of 4.00.

My research interests lie at the frontier of renewable energy, electrochemistry, nanotechnology, and computational chemistry. I am particularly passionate about exploring sustainable solutions in the energy sector through advanced chemical and computational approaches including DFT simulations.

Beyond academia, I am an active leader and team player — serving in multiple organizational roles and contributing to community-driven scientific outreach.
