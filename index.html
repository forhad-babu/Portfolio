About Me

Aspiring Researcher,
seeking opportunity

I am Md. Forhad Babu, a final-year undergraduate student of Applied Chemistry and Chemical Engineering (ACCE) at the University of Rajshahi, Bangladesh, maintaining a CGPA of 3.65 out of 4.00.

My primary research interests encompass renewable energy materials (Dye-Sensitized Solar Cells and Perovskite Solar Cells), computational chemistry (Density Functional Theory), electrochemistry and corrosion science, battery and energy storage systems, and nanomaterials for catalysis and energy applications. I am proficient in simulation tools including Gaussian, GaussView, Material Studio, CAPS1D, MATLAB, and OriginPro.

I have served in several leadership and organizational roles throughout my academic career, including as President of USASS, Vice President and IT & Media Secretary of RUHERF, and in executive capacities across multiple student associations. These experiences have strengthened my ability to lead, communicate, and collaborate in team environments.

My long-term professional goal is to contribute to sustainable energy solutions through advanced research and academia. To pursue this, I am actively seeking a fully-funded Master's or PhD program abroad, where I can work alongside leading researchers in energy materials and computational chemistry. As a student from a developing country with limited financial resources, a full scholarship is essential for me to access world-class research infrastructure and realize my academic potential.

I am driven by the conviction that science must serve society — and I am committed to building a research career that addresses pressing global challenges in clean energy and materials innovation.
